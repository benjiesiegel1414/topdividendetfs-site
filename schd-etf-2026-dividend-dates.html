When Does SCHD Pay Its Dividend in 2026?

The Schwab U.S. Dividend Equity ETF (SCHD) distributes dividends quarterly — four times per year. Below is the complete official SCHD ETF dividend date schedule for 2026, including every ex-dividend date, record date, and payable date, sourced directly from Schwab's 2026 ETF Distributions Schedule.

To receive a SCHD dividend payment, you must own shares before the ex-dividend date. If you purchase shares on or after the ex-date, you will not receive that quarter's dividend. SCHD typically pays its dividend approximately 5 business days after the ex-dividend date.

The Schwab U.S. Dividend Equity ETF (SCHD) is one of the most popular dividend ETFs in the United States, with tens of billions in assets under management. It tracks the Dow Jones U.S. Dividend 100 Index, which selects 100 high-dividend-yielding U.S. stocks screened for quality, financial strength, and dividend sustainability.

SCHD is a top choice for income investors thanks to its competitive dividend yield, very low expense ratio, and a portfolio of financially strong, dividend-growing U.S. companies. Its quarterly dividend schedule means investors receive predictable income four times per year.

How to Use the SCHD Dividend Schedule

Three key dates determine whether you qualify for a SCHD dividend and when you get paid.

Understanding the Three Key Dates

  • Ex-Dividend Date: You must own SCHD shares before this date to receive the dividend. Buying on or after the ex-date means you miss that quarter's payment.
  • Record Date: The date Schwab officially records eligible shareholders. For SCHD in 2026, the ex-date and record date are the same each quarter.
  • Payable Date: The date the dividend is deposited into your brokerage account — typically 5 business days after the ex-dividend date.
  1. Find the upcoming SCHD ex-dividend date Use the table above. You must hold shares before this date to qualify for that quarter's payment.
  2. Purchase shares before the ex-date Buy SCHD at least one trading day before the ex-dividend date so your trade settles in time.
  3. Hold through the record date Stay a shareholder on the record date (same day as ex-date for SCHD in 2026).
  4. Receive your payment on the payable date Your SCHD dividend is credited to your brokerage account approximately 5 days after the ex-date.

💡 Dividend Reinvestment (DRIP)

Most brokerages allow you to automatically reinvest your SCHD dividends back into additional shares via a Dividend Reinvestment Plan (DRIP). Reinvesting your quarterly SCHD dividends can significantly compound your total return over time.
