What is QQQI ETF?

QQQI is the NEOS Nasdaq-100 High Income ETF designed to generate high monthly income while maintaining exposure to the Nasdaq-100 Index.

The ETF utilizes a data-driven options strategy involving Nasdaq-100 index options that may include both sold and purchased call options — allowing investors to potentially capture upside participation while generating large monthly income distributions.

Frequently Asked Questions About QQQI

Why is QQQI’s newest payout important?

The May 2026 payout of $0.6589 marks the ETF’s largest monthly distribution ever since inception, highlighting strong option premium generation and growing investor interest.

How often does QQQI pay dividends?

QQQI distributes income monthly.

What strategy does QQQI use?

QQQI uses an actively managed Nasdaq-100 index options strategy designed to generate high monthly income while still maintaining upside participation potential.

Is QQQI tax efficient?

QQQI utilizes index options classified under Section 1256 contracts which may provide favorable tax treatment compared to traditional covered call ETFs.

Does QQQI include return of capital?

Yes. Some distributions may include return of capital alongside option premiums, dividends, interest and gains.
