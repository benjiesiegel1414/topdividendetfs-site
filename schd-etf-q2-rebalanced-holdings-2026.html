What Is the SCHD Quarterly Rebalance?

SCHD rebalances quarterly on the third Friday of June, September, and December. Unlike the annual reconstitution in March — which adds and removes stocks — the quarterly rebalance is primarily about redistributing weight across the existing portfolio. Positions that have grown above the 4% per-stock cap get trimmed, and that weight gets spread throughout the fund.

The Q2 2026 rebalance resulted in a significant rotation: Technology was cut from ~15% to ~10% of the fund, primarily by reducing $TXN and $QCOM, while Healthcare, Consumer Staples, and Energy all saw their allocations grow. The result is a more balanced top of the portfolio and a meaningfully more defensive tilt overall.

Key Takeaways for SCHD Investors

What the Q2 2026 rebalance means for your portfolio.

  • ✂️
    Tech exposure cut nearly in half TXN fell from 6.39% to 4.35% and QCOM dropped from 6.22% to 3.59%. Combined, those two positions shrank from 12.61% to 7.94% of the fund — a 4.67 percentage point reduction from just two stocks.
  • 🛡️
    Defensive dividend names received the freed-up weight ABT (+0.94%), HD (+0.59%), AMGN (+0.56%), PEP (+0.52%), PG (+0.46%), and MRK (+0.28%) were all meaningfully increased — all blue-chip names with strong dividend track records.
  • ⚖️
    Concentration risk lowered meaningfully Top 5 holdings went from 25.98% to 21.31% of assets. Top 10 went from 44.26% to 41.13%. The fund is now better diversified at the top than it has been in recent quarters.
  • 📊
    No single holding is above 4.36% Before this rebalance, SCHD had three holdings above 5.5%. The 4% per-stock cap is now effectively enforced across the entire top of the portfolio, making the fund more index-like and less dependent on any single name.
  • 🏥
    Healthcare and Staples now each at 20% — the fund's two largest sectors Both sectors grew by one percentage point, reinforcing SCHD's reputation as a defensive, income-oriented ETF rather than a tech-heavy dividend play.
