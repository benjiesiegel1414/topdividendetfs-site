What Is CHPY?

$CHPY is the YieldMax Semiconductor Portfolio Option Income ETF. It launched on April 2, 2025 and trades on NYSE Arca. Instead of tracking one stock, it holds a basket of roughly 15 to 30 semiconductor companies and sells call spreads on them to generate weekly income. Top holdings have included $NVDA, $AMD, $MRVL, $LRCX, $INTC, $MU and $AVGO.

Since launch it has paid 78 straight weekly dividends totaling $38.02 per share. Payouts averaged about $0.40 in its first 13 weeks and about $0.56 over the latest 13 weeks. The 2025 payouts added up to $15.68 per share across 38 weeks, and 2026 has delivered $22.35 across 40 weeks so far.

Keep in mind: YieldMax estimates that a large share of CHPY distributions is return of capital, the fund has a 1.03% gross expense ratio, and option income strategies give up some upside when chips rally hard. The stated distribution rate (42.55% on October 6, 2026) annualizes one week and is not a forecast.
