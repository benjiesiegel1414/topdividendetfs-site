✅ Q2 2026 Dividend Announced — $0.2525 Per Share

SCHD has officially declared its Q2 2026 dividend of $0.2525 per share. To receive this payment, you must own shares before the ex-dividend date of June 24, 2026. The payment date is June 29, 2026. This brings the trailing 12-month payout to approximately $1.05 per share.
