Eight SCHD Holdings Are Already Paying More in Q4, and Two of Them Are Top 20 Positions

Q4 is SCHD's biggest dividend quarter of the year more often than not, and the raises feeding it are already rolling in. If you own the Schwab U.S. Dividend Equity ETF ($SCHD), here's why that matters: SCHD doesn't set its own dividend. It collects the cash dividends paid by its roughly 100 holdings and passes that income through to shareholders. Every raise a holding pays before SCHD's December ex-date is more cash in the Q4 pot.

So far, eight SCHD holdings are paying higher dividends in Q4 2026: City Holding ($CHCO) +14.9%, American Financial Group ($AFG) +10.2%, Texas Instruments ($TXN) +7.0%, Fifth Third ($FITB) +5.0%, Ennis ($EBF) +5.0%, Accenture ($ACN) +4.9%, Bank OZK ($OZK) +2.1%, and Virtus Investment Partners ($VRTS) +2.1%. We verified every number against each company's own announcement. Full sources, including SCHD Stan (Craig) on X, who has been logging these raises as they hit, are listed at the bottom of this page.

📌 How we define a "Q4 raise"

This tracker counts a raise in the quarter when SCHD actually collects the bigger check, not the day it's announced. $TXN and $VRTS announced their increases in Q3 (both appear on our Q3 2026 tracker), but their first higher payments land on November 10 and November 13. That cash belongs to SCHD's Q4 distribution, so it belongs here too.

The headline percentages are led by small banks and insurers. The income is led by two tech names. Texas Instruments and Accenture alone are about 7.3 of the 8.9 percentage points of fund weight raising, and Texas Instruments got even more important after September's rebalance pushed it to SCHD's #2 holding.

Two names do almost all of the work. $TXN and $ACN account for roughly 82% of the weighted lift, while the six financials and industrials split the rest. $CHCO's 14.9% raise is the best headline on the board, yet it contributes about a fortieth of what $TXN does. That's the recurring lesson of every SCHD tracker: the exciting percentages usually come from tiny positions.

📈 The rebalance made $TXN's raise worth more

When Texas Instruments announced its 7% raise on September 17, it was about a 3.2% position. The September rebalance then lifted it to 4.41%, SCHD's #2 holding behind QUALCOMM ($QCOM). Same raise, about 37% more weight behind it. SCHD now holds roughly 17.9 million $TXN shares, so the extra $0.10 per share works out to about $1.79 million more cash per quarter from one company.

AFG announced a 10.2% increase in its annual dividend rate back in August, taking the quarterly payout to $0.97. It's the insurer's 21st consecutive year of dividend increases. The first higher check is paid October 23 to holders of record October 15.

Q4 2026 Cuts Watch

✅ No Q4 cuts tracked yet

No SCHD holding has announced a dividend cut or suspension for Q4 so far. Last quarter we tracked two: Wendy's ($WEN) cut 50% and Skyworks ($SWKS) suspended its dividend, both covered on the Q3 tracker. If any cuts show up this quarter, they'll be added here, because a tracker that only counts good news isn't a tracker.

What Could This Mean for SCHD's Q4 2026 Payout?

Q4 has a habit of being SCHD's strongest quarter. Last year SCHD paid $0.2782 in Q4 2025, its largest payout of 2025, up from $0.2645 in Q4 2024 (split-adjusted). The most recent payout, $0.2665 in Q3 2026, was up 2.3% year over year and 5.5% over Q2. See the full breakdown in our Q3 2026 dividend announcement post.

Put the tracker in dollar terms and the eight raises add about $3.9 million in extra dividend cash per quarter. Spread across SCHD's billions of shares outstanding, that's on the order of a tenth of a cent per SCHD share, or around 0.4% of a typical quarterly payout. Real money, and it compounds, but it isn't a jackpot on its own. Q4 raise season is just getting started, and many SCHD holdings typically announce their increases in October through December.

⚠️ Important: Raises Don't Guarantee a Bigger SCHD Dividend

SCHD's payout also depends on share creation and redemption activity, the September rebalance's sector shifts, any dividend cuts still to come, special dividends, and the timing of when holding dividends are collected. Eight raises are a positive signal, not a promise. Always wait for the official declaration from Schwab Asset Management.

The Bigger Picture: Why Dividend Growth Investors Watch These Announcements

SCHD tracks the Dow Jones U.S. Dividend 100, which screens for at least 10 consecutive years of dividend payments, strong free cash flow, high return on equity, and dividend growth. Quarters like this one show that screen at work: Texas Instruments on its 23rd straight annual raise, American Financial Group on its 21st, Fifth Third on its 11th, and Bank OZK raising every single quarter for 16 years.

SCHD Q4 2026 Dividend Tracker FAQ

How many SCHD holdings have raised their dividend for Q4 2026?

All eight pay their first higher dividend before SCHD's expected December 10 ex-date.

Which Q4 raise matters most for SCHD's dividend?

Texas Instruments ($TXN). Its 7% raise sits on a 4.41% weight, making it SCHD's #2 holding after the September rebalance. It adds roughly $1.79 million of extra dividend cash to SCHD per quarter, more than any other raise on this list. Accenture ($ACN) is second at about $1.36 million per quarter.

Did Accenture raise its dividend in 2026?

Yes. On October 1, 2026, Accenture raised its quarterly dividend 4.9% from $1.63 to $1.71 per share ($6.84 annualized), payable November 13, 2026 to shareholders of record October 13. Accenture is about a 2.86% weight in SCHD.

What is the biggest SCHD holding dividend increase for Q4 2026?

By percentage, City Holding Company ($CHCO) at +14.9%, raising its quarterly dividend from $0.87 to $1.00. By impact on SCHD's income, Texas Instruments ($TXN) is far larger because it is a much bigger position in the fund.

Why are Texas Instruments and Virtus on both the Q3 and Q4 trackers?

Both announced their raises in Q3, but their first higher payments are in November (TXN on November 10, VRTS on November 13). We count a raise in the quarter SCHD actually collects the higher cash, so their income impact shows up in SCHD's Q4 distribution.

When is SCHD's Q4 2026 dividend paid?

SCHD's Q4 2026 ex-dividend date is expected on December 10, 2026, with payment on December 15, 2026. You need to own shares before the ex-dividend date to receive the Q4 payout. Schwab confirms the exact amount when it declares the dividend.

How much did SCHD pay in Q4 2025?

SCHD paid $0.2782 per share in Q4 2025, its largest payout of 2025 and up 5.2% from Q4 2024's $0.2645 (split-adjusted). The most recent payout was $0.2665 in Q3 2026.

Do these raises guarantee a higher SCHD dividend in Q4?

No. Raises are a positive signal, but SCHD's payout also depends on share creation and redemption, rebalance changes, cuts, special dividends, and collection timing. Together the eight raises add about $3.9 million per quarter, roughly a tenth of a cent per SCHD share. Wait for Schwab's official declaration.

Has any SCHD holding cut its dividend in Q4 2026?

Not so far. Last quarter Wendy's ($WEN) cut its dividend 50% and Skyworks ($SWKS) suspended it. This tracker will add any Q4 cuts as they are announced.

How long has Bank OZK been raising its dividend?

Bank OZK's October 2026 increase to $0.49 per share was its 65th consecutive quarterly dividend increase, more than 16 straight years of raising every quarter.
