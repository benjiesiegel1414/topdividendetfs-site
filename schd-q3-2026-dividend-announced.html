Schwab Just Declared SCHD's Q3 2026 Dividend

The number SCHD investors have been waiting on all quarter is finally here. On September 22, 2026, Schwab Asset Management declared the Q3 2026 distribution for the Schwab U.S. Dividend Equity ETF ($SCHD): $0.2665 per share.

That payout is higher than last year's Q3 dividend of $0.2604 (+2.3%) and up from the Q2 2026 payout of $0.2525 (+5.5%). For income investors, the year-over-year comparison is the one that matters most, because SCHD's payouts swing quarter to quarter based on when its roughly 100 holdings pay their own dividends.

The timing is tight this quarter. The ex-dividend date is tomorrow, Wednesday, September 23. If you want this payout, you need to own SCHD before the opening bell on the 23rd. Buy on the ex-date or later and the Q3 dividend goes to the seller.

What Drove SCHD's Q3 2026 Dividend?

SCHD is a pass-through fund. It collects the dividends paid by the companies it owns during the quarter, subtracts its tiny 0.06% expense ratio, and passes the rest to shareholders. So the Q3 number is really a report card on the portfolio.

The tailwinds: We tracked 17 SCHD holdings that raised their dividends this quarter, led by Regions Financial ($RF) at +13.2%, Broadridge ($BR) at +11.8%, and Blackstone ($BX) at +11.2%. Heavier positions like Altria ($MO, +4.7%) matter more than the headline percentages suggest, since what moves SCHD's payout is weight multiplied by raise. Ethan Allen ($ETD) also paid a $3.00 special dividend.

The headwinds: Wendy's ($WEN) cut its dividend 50% and Skyworks ($SWKS) suspended its payout entirely. Both are small weights, but they pull in the other direction. New share creation also dilutes the income pool, because more shares outstanding means the collected cash is split more ways.

The timing effect: Not every raise lands in Q3. Texas Instruments' ($TXN) +7% raise, the heaviest raiser by weight this quarter, first pays on November 10. That extra cash shows up in SCHD's Q4 distribution, not this one.

The next SCHD dividend is the Q4 2026 payout, with an expected ex-dividend date of December 10 and a pay date of December 15. Q4 has historically been one of SCHD's bigger quarters, and this year it gets an extra boost baked in: Texas Instruments' raise, the largest by weight among this quarter's announcements, starts paying in November and lands squarely in the Q4 collection window.

SCHD's distributions depend on the dividends its holdings actually pay, index reconstitution, sector weights, and share creation or redemption activity. Quarterly payouts can and do fall even in years when the annual total rises. Always confirm official figures with Schwab before making decisions.

SCHD Q3 2026 Dividend FAQ

How much is SCHD's Q3 2026 dividend?

$0.2665 per share, declared September 22, 2026. That is +2.3% versus Q3 2025 ($0.2604) and +5.5% versus Q2 2026 ($0.2525).

When is the SCHD Q3 2026 ex-dividend date?

Wednesday, September 23, 2026, which is also the record date. You must own SCHD before the market opens on September 23 to receive this dividend.

When does SCHD pay its Q3 2026 dividend?

Monday, September 28, 2026. Most brokerages credit the cash (or reinvested shares, if you use DRIP) that day.

How much will 1,000 shares of SCHD pay in Q3 2026?

1,000 shares × $0.2665 = $266.50 before taxes. Use the SCHD Dividend Calculator for your exact share count.

What is SCHD's trailing 12-month dividend now?

$1.0541 per share: Q4 2025 ($0.2782) + Q1 2026 ($0.2569) + Q2 2026 ($0.2525) + Q3 2026 ($0.2665).

Why doesn't SCHD pay the same dividend every quarter?

SCHD passes through the dividends its holdings pay during each quarter. Payment timing, holding raises and cuts, special dividends, index rebalancing, and share creation all shift the total from quarter to quarter. That is why the year-over-year comparison tells you more than the quarter-over-quarter one.

When is SCHD's next dividend?

Q4 2026, with an expected ex-date of December 10 and pay date of December 15. See the full 2026 SCHD dividend calendar.
