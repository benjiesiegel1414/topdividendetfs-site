Which Amplify ETF Should You Pick?

If you want a proven income fund, $DIVO and its international sibling $IDVO have the best track records here.

If you want more yield without price decay so far, $QDVO, $HCOW and $HAKY are options, though $HCOW and $HAKY are very small.

The Bitcoin funds and $YYY pay the most but show price decay on our list. $BATT and $COWS aren't really income funds.

This is educational, not a recommendation. The right fund depends on your goals, your taxes and how much volatility you can stomach.

The Risks I'd Want You to Know

  • Price decay. Our list flags price decay on $BAGY, $BITY, $YYY, $BATT. A big yield doesn't help if the share price keeps sliding.
  • Capped upside on option funds. Selling calls trades away part of the upside for income, so covered call funds lag in big rallies.
  • Variable payouts. Option income funds pay different amounts each period. Budget off the yearly total, not one big check.
  • Short history. $HAKY has less than a year of data.
  • Small funds. $BAGY, $BITY, $HAKY, $HCOW, $COWS have under $50 million in assets, which can mean wider trading spreads and closure risk.
  • Taxes. Option income is often taxed as ordinary income, and some payouts are return of capital. Check your 1099 and talk to a tax pro.

My Takeaway

If you're searching for Amplify's highest yielding ETF, the answer is $BAGY at roughly 33%, paid monthly, but with price decay on our list. The best total return since launch in the group belongs to $DIVO at +225%.

Here's how I think about it. I don't buy an income ETF because of the yield number alone. I want three things: reliable payouts, no price decay, and a total return I can live with. A 10% yield that keeps your principal intact beats a 40% yield that slowly melts.
