BIGY FAQ

    At a 12% yield, $10,000 invested in $BIGY would pay roughly $1,200 per year, or about $100 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.
  • What grade does BIGY get?+
    $BIGY earns a A- on the TopDividendETFs.com scorecard, one of the highest grades on our list. It ranks 52nd of 198 when every ETF is sorted by grade. The grade is our own rating and is not a recommendation to buy or sell.

    $BIGY is flagged for price decay, meaning its share price has trended down over time as it pays out high income. Total return matters more than yield alone for funds like this.
