📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $BALI or unusually high or low.

Listed yield6.6%The yield shown on the TopDividendETFs list.
Trailing 12-month yield8.0%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate6.7%The latest payout times 12, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$BALI Yield Trend FAQ

What is $BALI's yield right now?

$BALI's trailing 12-month yield is 8.0% at a share price of $33.08 (Nov 16, 2026). Its listed yield on TopDividendETFs is 6.6%.

Is $BALI's yield going up or down?

It was 8.4% a year ago and is 8.0% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed +0.1% and its share price changed +4.9%.

Why are there different yield numbers for $BALI?

The listed yield is the figure on our main list, the trailing yield uses what it actually paid over 12 months, and the distribution rate uses only the latest payout times 12. They split apart when payouts or the price move quickly.
