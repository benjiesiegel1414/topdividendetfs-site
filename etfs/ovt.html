OVT FAQ

    At a 6.6% yield, $10,000 invested in $OVT would pay roughly $660 per year, or about $55 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.
  • What grade does OVT get?+
    $OVT earns a B- on the TopDividendETFs.com scorecard, a solid grade on our list. It ranks 105th of 198 when every ETF is sorted by grade. The grade is our own rating and is not a recommendation to buy or sell.

    $OVT is flagged for price decay, meaning its share price has trended down over time as it pays out high income. Total return matters more than yield alone for funds like this.

    $OVT has about $62M in assets under management, making it the 124th largest of the 198 ETFs we track.
