NIHI FAQ

    At a 10.2% yield, $10,000 invested in $NIHI would pay roughly $1,020 per year, or about $85 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.

    $NIHI earns a A- on the TopDividendETFs.com scorecard, one of the highest grades on our list. It ranks 38th of 198 when every ETF is sorted by grade. The grade is our own rating and is not a recommendation to buy or sell.

    $NIHI is flagged for price decay, meaning its share price has trended down over time as it pays out high income. Total return matters more than yield alone for funds like this.
