📚 What Price Decay Means

Price decay, also called NAV erosion, means a fund's share price has generally declined since inception. It is common with very high yield funds that pay out more than they earn: each payout shrinks the price a little, and over time the shares are worth less.

What is price decay (NAV erosion)?

Price decay means a fund's share price has generally declined since inception. It usually happens when a fund pays out more than it earns, so part of each payout comes out of the share price. It refers to the price only, not total return.
