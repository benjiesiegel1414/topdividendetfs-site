📚 How the $SNOY Dividend Calendar Works

Ex-dividend date: the cutoff. Own $SNOY before this date to get the payout. Buy on or after it and the payout goes to the seller.

Pay date: when the cash lands in your account, usually a few days after the ex-date.

$SNOY pays weekly, so the next ex-date should land around Nov 19, 2026. Funds announce the exact date shortly before, so treat this as a guide.
