TSII FAQ

  • What is TSII's dividend yield?+

    At a 61% yield, $10,000 invested in $TSII would pay roughly $6,100 per year, or about $508 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.

    $TSII earns a D+ on the TopDividendETFs.com scorecard, one of the lower grades on our list. It ranks 148th of 198 when every ETF is sorted by grade. The grade is our own rating and is not a recommendation to buy or sell.

    $TSII is flagged for price decay, meaning its share price has trended down over time as it pays out high income. Total return matters more than yield alone for funds like this.

    $TSII has about $29M in assets under management, making it the 147th largest of the 198 ETFs we track.
