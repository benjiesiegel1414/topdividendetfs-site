📚 Reading $XPAY's Dividend History

The dividend history lists every cash payout $XPAY has made per share, by ex-dividend date. You get the payout if you own shares before the ex-date.

Many income ETFs, especially option income funds, set each payout from what the fund earned, so the amount moves around. Look at the trend more than any single payout: a steady or rising line is a good sign, a line that keeps stepping down usually means less income ahead.
