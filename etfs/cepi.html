CEPI FAQ

  • What is CEPI's dividend yield?+
    $CEPI (REX Crypto Equity Premium Income ETF) currently shows a dividend yield of about 42.2% on TopDividendETFs.com. That ranks 29th highest out of the 198 dividend ETFs we track and is higher than 85% of them. Yields change with the share price and each distribution.
  • How much would $10,000 in CEPI pay?+
    At a 42.2% yield, $10,000 invested in $CEPI would pay roughly $4,220 per year, or about $352 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.
  • What grade does CEPI get?+
    $CEPI earns a F on the TopDividendETFs.com scorecard, one of the lower grades on our list. It ranks 162nd of 198 when every ETF is sorted by grade. The grade is our own rating and is not a recommendation to buy or sell.
  • Does CEPI have price decay?+
    $CEPI is flagged for price decay, meaning its share price has trended down over time as it pays out high income. Total return matters more than yield alone for funds like this.
  • How big is CEPI?+
    $CEPI has about $112M in assets under management, making it the 102nd largest of the 198 ETFs we track.
  • Who manages CEPI?+
    $CEPI is part of the REX lineup. We track 6 REX ETFs on TopDividendETFs.com.
  • What ETFs are similar to CEPI?+
    ETFs in the same crypto income group with a similar yield include $XBCI (41%), $YBIT (41%), $LFGY (40.8%), $YBTC (38.6%).
