DVY FAQ

  • What is DVY's dividend yield?+
    $DVY (iShares Select Dividend ETF) currently shows a dividend yield of about 3.5% on TopDividendETFs.com. That ranks 181st highest out of the 198 dividend ETFs we track and is higher than 9% of them. Yields change with the share price and each distribution.

    At a 3.5% yield, $10,000 invested in $DVY would pay roughly $350 per year, or about $29 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.
  • What grade does DVY get?+
    $DVY earns a A on the TopDividendETFs.com scorecard, one of the highest grades on our list. It ranks 11th of 198 when every ETF is sorted by grade. The grade is our own rating and is not a recommendation to buy or sell.
  • Does DVY have price decay?+
    $DVY is not flagged for price decay on our list, meaning its share price has generally held up while it pays income.
  • How big is DVY?+
    $DVY has about $23.6B in assets under management, making it the 10th largest of the 198 ETFs we track.
