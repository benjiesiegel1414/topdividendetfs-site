📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $VYMI or unusually high or low.

Listed yield3.7%The yield shown on the TopDividendETFs list.
Trailing 12-month yield3.7%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate3.2%The latest payout times 4, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$VYMI Yield Trend FAQ

What is $VYMI's yield right now?

$VYMI's trailing 12-month yield is 3.7% at a share price of $101.23 (Nov 16, 2026). Its listed yield on TopDividendETFs is 3.7%.

Is $VYMI's yield going up or down?

It was 3.8% a year ago and is 3.7% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed +11.4% and its share price changed +15.6%.

Why are there different yield numbers for $VYMI?
