YYY FAQ

  • What is YYY's dividend yield?+
    $YYY (Amplify High Income ETF) currently shows a dividend yield of about 13.9% on TopDividendETFs.com. That ranks 111th highest out of the 198 dividend ETFs we track and is higher than 44% of them. Yields change with the share price and each distribution.
  • How much would $10,000 in YYY pay?+
    At a 13.9% yield, $10,000 invested in $YYY would pay roughly $1,390 per year, or about $116 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.
  • What grade does YYY get?+
    $YYY earns a D+ on the TopDividendETFs.com scorecard, one of the lower grades on our list. It ranks 139th of 198 when every ETF is sorted by grade. The grade is our own rating and is not a recommendation to buy or sell.
  • Does YYY have price decay?+
    $YYY is flagged for price decay, meaning its share price has trended down over time as it pays out high income. Total return matters more than yield alone for funds like this.
  • How big is YYY?+
    $YYY has about $618M in assets under management, making it the 55th largest of the 198 ETFs we track.
  • Who manages YYY?+
