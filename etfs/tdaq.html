TDAQ FAQ

  • What is TDAQ's dividend yield?+
    $TDAQ (TappAlpha Innovation 100 Growth & Daily Income ETF) currently shows a dividend yield of about 17.6% on TopDividendETFs.com. That ranks 93rd highest out of the 198 dividend ETFs we track and is higher than 53% of them. Yields change with the share price and each distribution.

    At a 17.6% yield, $10,000 invested in $TDAQ would pay roughly $1,760 per year, or about $147 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.
  • What grade does TDAQ get?+
    $TDAQ earns a A- on the TopDividendETFs.com scorecard, one of the highest grades on our list. It ranks 34th of 198 when every ETF is sorted by grade. The grade is our own rating and is not a recommendation to buy or sell.
  • Does TDAQ have price decay?+
    $TDAQ is flagged for price decay, meaning its share price has trended down over time as it pays out high income. Total return matters more than yield alone for funds like this.
  • How big is TDAQ?+
    $TDAQ has about $500M in assets under management, making it the 64th largest of the 198 ETFs we track.
  • Who manages TDAQ?+
