TSPY FAQ

    At a 14.5% yield, $10,000 invested in $TSPY would pay roughly $1,450 per year, or about $121 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.
  • What grade does TSPY get?+

    $TSPY is flagged for price decay, meaning its share price has trended down over time as it pays out high income. Total return matters more than yield alone for funds like this.
