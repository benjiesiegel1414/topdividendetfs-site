📚 What Total Return Tells You

Total return is the full result of owning $SPYH: the change in the share price plus every dividend, reinvested. It is the fairest way to compare an income fund with anything else.

A high yield with a weak total return means the price is giving back much of what the fund pays out. A total return that keeps up with the yield means the fund is paying you without eating into the price.

What is the difference between total return and price return?

Price return only counts the share price. Total return also counts the dividends. For $SPYH, dividends added about +8.2% on top of the price move over the last year.
