BITO FAQ

  • What is BITO's dividend yield?+
    $BITO (ProShares Bitcoin ETF) currently shows a dividend yield of about 38.2% on TopDividendETFs.com. That ranks 46th highest out of the 198 dividend ETFs we track and is higher than 77% of them. Yields change with the share price and each distribution.
  • How much would $10,000 in BITO pay?+
    At a 38.2% yield, $10,000 invested in $BITO would pay roughly $3,820 per year, or about $318 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.
  • What grade does BITO get?+
    $BITO earns a F on the TopDividendETFs.com scorecard, one of the lower grades on our list. It ranks 156th of 198 when every ETF is sorted by grade. The grade is our own rating and is not a recommendation to buy or sell.
  • Does BITO have price decay?+
    $BITO is flagged for price decay, meaning its share price has trended down over time as it pays out high income. Total return matters more than yield alone for funds like this.
  • How big is BITO?+
    $BITO has about $1.2B in assets under management, making it the 40th largest of the 198 ETFs we track.
  • Who manages BITO?+
