BITY FAQ

  • What is BITY's dividend yield?+
    $BITY (Amplify Bitcoin 2% Monthly Option Income ETF) currently shows a dividend yield of about 26.5% on TopDividendETFs.com. That ranks 72nd highest out of the 198 dividend ETFs we track and is higher than 64% of them. Yields change with the share price and each distribution.

    At a 26.5% yield, $10,000 invested in $BITY would pay roughly $2,650 per year, or about $221 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.
  • What grade does BITY get?+
    $BITY earns a F on the TopDividendETFs.com scorecard, one of the lower grades on our list. It ranks 182nd of 198 when every ETF is sorted by grade. The grade is our own rating and is not a recommendation to buy or sell.
  • Does BITY have price decay?+
    $BITY is flagged for price decay, meaning its share price has trended down over time as it pays out high income. Total return matters more than yield alone for funds like this.
  • How big is BITY?+
    $BITY has about $7.6M in assets under management, making it the 175th largest of the 198 ETFs we track.
