📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $DGRO or unusually high or low.

Listed yield2%The yield shown on the TopDividendETFs list.
Trailing 12-month yield1.9%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate2.0%The latest payout times 4, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$DGRO Yield Trend FAQ

What is $DGRO's yield right now?

$DGRO's trailing 12-month yield is 1.9% at a share price of $78.70 (Nov 19, 2026). Its listed yield on TopDividendETFs is 2%.

Is $DGRO's yield going up or down?

It was 2.0% a year ago and is 1.9% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed +8.1% and its share price changed +16.2%.

Why are there different yield numbers for $DGRO?
