    At a 21% yield, $10,000 invested in $SVOL would pay roughly $2,100 per year, or about $175 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.

    $SVOL is flagged for price decay, meaning its share price has trended down over time as it pays out high income. Total return matters more than yield alone for funds like this.
