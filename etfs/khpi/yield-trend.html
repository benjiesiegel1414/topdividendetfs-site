📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $KHPI or unusually high or low.

Listed yield9%The yield shown on the TopDividendETFs list.
Trailing 12-month yield9.4%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate9.5%The latest payout times 12, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$KHPI Yield Trend FAQ

What is $KHPI's yield right now?

$KHPI's trailing 12-month yield is 9.4% at a share price of $24.70 (Nov 20, 2026). Its listed yield on TopDividendETFs is 9%.

Is $KHPI's yield going up or down?

It was 8.9% a year ago and is 9.4% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed +1.8% and its share price changed -3.7%.

What is the highest yield $KHPI has had?

9.8% on Oct 30, 2026, when the share price was $25.76. Its lowest was 8.7% on Oct 24, 2025.

Why are there different yield numbers for $KHPI?

The listed yield is the figure on our main list, the trailing yield uses what it actually paid over 12 months, and the distribution rate uses only the latest payout times 12. They split apart when payouts or the price move quickly.
