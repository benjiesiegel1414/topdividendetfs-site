BTCI FAQ

  • What is BTCI's dividend yield?+
    $BTCI (NEOS Bitcoin High Income ETF) currently shows a dividend yield of about 25% on TopDividendETFs.com. That ranks 71st highest out of the 198 dividend ETFs we track and is higher than 63% of them. Yields change with the share price and each distribution.
  • How much would $10,000 in BTCI pay?+
    At a 25% yield, $10,000 invested in $BTCI would pay roughly $2,500 per year, or about $208 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.
  • What grade does BTCI get?+
    $BTCI earns a D+ on the TopDividendETFs.com scorecard, one of the lower grades on our list. It ranks 138th of 198 when every ETF is sorted by grade. The grade is our own rating and is not a recommendation to buy or sell.
  • Does BTCI have price decay?+
    $BTCI is flagged for price decay, meaning its share price has trended down over time as it pays out high income. Total return matters more than yield alone for funds like this.
  • How big is BTCI?+
    $BTCI has about $1.3B in assets under management, making it the 43rd largest of the 198 ETFs we track.
  • Who manages BTCI?+
    $BTCI is part of the NEOS lineup. We track 15 NEOS ETFs on TopDividendETFs.com.
  • What ETFs are similar to BTCI?+
    ETFs in the same crypto income group with a similar yield include $BITY (26.5%), $ISBG (21%), $XBTY (31%), $ISSB (18%).
