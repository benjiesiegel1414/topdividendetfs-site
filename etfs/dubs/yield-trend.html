📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $DUBS or unusually high or low.

Listed yield2%The yield shown on the TopDividendETFs list.
Trailing 12-month yield2.1%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate2.3%The latest payout times 4, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$DUBS Yield Trend FAQ

What is $DUBS's yield right now?

Is $DUBS's yield going up or down?

It was 2.2% a year ago and is 2.1% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed +7.0% and its share price changed +11.5%.
