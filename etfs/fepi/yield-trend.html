📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $FEPI or unusually high or low.

Listed yield25.2%The yield shown on the TopDividendETFs list.
Trailing 12-month yield30.1%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate5.9%The latest payout times 12, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$FEPI Yield Trend FAQ

What is $FEPI's yield right now?

$FEPI's trailing 12-month yield is 30.1% at a share price of $36.03 (Nov 19, 2026). Its listed yield on TopDividendETFs is 25.2%.

Is $FEPI's yield going up or down?

It was 26.2% a year ago and is 30.1% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed -8.6% and its share price changed -20.2%.

Why are there different yield numbers for $FEPI?
