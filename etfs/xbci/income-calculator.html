📚 How This Calculator Works

We take the amount you invest, divide by $XBCI's latest closing price to get your shares, then multiply by the latest monthly payout of $1.117 per share, 12 times a year, and show it as a monthly average. The 12-month average payout is $1.2985, so the "At 12-Mo Avg" column gives a smoother estimate.

Keep in mind: payouts change, the share price moves every day, and taxes come out of what you receive in a regular brokerage account. Reinvesting the payouts instead of spending them buys more shares, which grows future income. See what that did over time on the $XBCI growth of $10,000 page.
