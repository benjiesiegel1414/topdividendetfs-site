📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $DIVB or unusually high or low.

Listed yield2.1%The yield shown on the TopDividendETFs list.
Trailing 12-month yield2.2%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate2.5%The latest payout times 4, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$DIVB Yield Trend FAQ

What is $DIVB's yield right now?

$DIVB's trailing 12-month yield is 2.2% at a share price of $67.44 (Nov 20, 2026). Its listed yield on TopDividendETFs is 2.1%.

Is $DIVB's yield going up or down?

It was 2.5% a year ago and is 2.2% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed +14.9% and its share price changed +33.3%.

Why are there different yield numbers for $DIVB?
