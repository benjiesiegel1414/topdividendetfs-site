    At a 6.2% yield, $10,000 invested in $OVB would pay roughly $620 per year, or about $52 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.
  • What grade does OVB get?+
    $OVB earns a C on the TopDividendETFs.com scorecard, a middle-of-the-pack grade on our list. It ranks 126th of 198 when every ETF is sorted by grade. The grade is our own rating and is not a recommendation to buy or sell.

    $OVB is flagged for price decay, meaning its share price has trended down over time as it pays out high income. Total return matters more than yield alone for funds like this.
