OVM FAQ

    At a 5.3% yield, $10,000 invested in $OVM would pay roughly $530 per year, or about $44 per month on average. Actual payouts vary, so treat this as an estimate based on the current yield.
  • What grade does OVM get?+
    $OVM earns a B- on the TopDividendETFs.com scorecard, a solid grade on our list. It ranks 107th of 198 when every ETF is sorted by grade. The grade is our own rating and is not a recommendation to buy or sell.

    $OVM is flagged for price decay, meaning its share price has trended down over time as it pays out high income. Total return matters more than yield alone for funds like this.

    $OVM has about $46M in assets under management, making it the 137th largest of the 198 ETFs we track.
