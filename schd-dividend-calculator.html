Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the SCHD dividend growth projection?

The calculator uses your selected growth rate (7–11%). Historical SCHD dividend growth has averaged ~9–11% annually over the past decade, but future growth is not guaranteed and depends on underlying holdings, market conditions, and economic factors.

Why show quarterly instead of monthly income?

SCHD pays dividends quarterly (typically March, June, September, December), so quarterly figures better reflect actual cash flow timing. If you prefer monthly income, check out our MonthlyETFs.com guide.

Are 30-year projections realistic?

Long-term projections are illustrative only. Dividend cuts, recessions, changes in fund composition, or inflation can significantly alter outcomes. Use this tool for education and planning — not as investment advice.

Where does the current dividend data come from?

The default $1.06 annual dividend (TTM) is based on the most recent trailing twelve months as of April 2026. The latest quarterly dividend (Q1 2026) was $0.2569. You can manually update the annual figure if newer data becomes available.
