⚠️ Why JEPQ Has No January Ex-Dividend Date

Scan the table above and January appears to be missing every year. It isn't — JEPQ runs two December ex-dividend dates annually. One falls at the start of December as normal, and a second falls in the final days of the month. That late-December distribution has a pay date in early January, and it is the payment shareholders receive as their January income.

  • Ex Dec 29, 2022 → paid Jan 4, 2023 ($0.57554)
  • Ex Dec 28, 2023 → paid Jan 3, 2024 ($0.39248)
  • Ex Dec 31, 2024 → paid Jan 3, 2025 ($0.45584)
  • Ex Dec 31, 2025 → paid Jan 5, 2026 ($0.57610)

If you buy JEPQ in early January expecting to catch a January ex-date, you have already missed it by roughly a week. Expect the same structure at the end of 2026.

JEPQ Dividend History FAQ

What was the latest JEPQ dividend?

JEPQ's most recent distribution is $0.68255 per share with an ex-dividend date of September 1, 2026 and a pay date of September 3, 2026. It is the second-largest monthly payout in the fund's history, behind only the record $0.70497 paid in August 2026, and about 3.2 percent below that August figure.

How many dividends has JEPQ paid?

JEPQ has paid 52 consecutive monthly distributions since inception on May 3, 2022, totaling $25.1319 per share at an average of $0.4833 per payment. It has never missed or skipped a month.

What is the highest dividend JEPQ has ever paid?

The highest was $0.70497 per share in August 2026, the first time the fund has ever paid above $0.70. September 2026 came in second at $0.68255, just ahead of the $0.68125 paid in November 2022. The lowest was $0.33975 in July 2022, its second month of existence.

Why does JEPQ have no January ex-dividend date?

JEPQ runs two December ex-dividend dates each year — one at the start of December and one in the final days. The late-December distribution pays in early January, covering the January payment. This has occurred every year since 2022.

How often does JEPQ pay dividends?

JEPQ pays monthly. The ex-dividend date is normally the first business day of the month, with the record date the same day and the pay date about two business days later.

What is the current JEPQ dividend yield?

JEPQ has paid $6.76 per share over the trailing twelve months, a yield of roughly 11 percent at the current share price. The exact yield fluctuates monthly based on option premiums and market volatility.

When was JEPQ launched?

JEPQ's inception date is May 3, 2022.

What is the difference between JEPQ and JEPI?

JEPI is built on the S&P 500 and is more defensive, while JEPQ is built on the Nasdaq 100 and is more technology-weighted. JEPQ generally provides a higher yield but with greater volatility.

Is JEPQ a good high yield monthly dividend ETF?

Many income investors use JEPQ for its monthly payouts and roughly 11 percent yield. Like other covered call strategies, it may experience NAV erosion in strong bull markets because the sold call options cap upside participation. It suits income-first investors rather than growth-focused accumulators.
