Dividend Stock FAQ

What is a Dividend King?

A Dividend King is a company that has raised its dividend every year for at least 50 years in a row, like $PG, $KO and $JNJ. Use the Dividend Kings quick view above to see all of them.

What is a Dividend Aristocrat?

A Dividend Aristocrat is an S&P 500 company that has raised its dividend every year for at least 25 years in a row. Some Aristocrats are also Kings.

How is the dividend yield calculated?

We take the most recent regular dividend, multiply it by how many times the company pays per year, and divide by the latest share price. One-time special dividends are left out so the yield is not inflated.

What does Price Decay mean for a stock?

Price Decay is YES when the share price today is lower than it was 5 years ago. It only looks at the share price, not dividends, and it is not a buy or sell signal.

How often is this list updated?
