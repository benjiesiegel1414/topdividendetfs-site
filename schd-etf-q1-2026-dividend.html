💡 What This Means

SCHD's Q1 2026 dividend of $0.2569 is up from Q1 2025's $0.2488 — a year-over-year increase of approximately 3.25% for the first quarter.

Note that Q1 dividends are historically the lowest of the four quarters for SCHD, with Q2 and Q4 typically being the largest. The full-year 2026 annualized dividend will become clearer as remaining quarters are declared.

SCHD's long-term dividend CAGR since 2012 is approximately ~11%, making it one of the top dividend growth ETFs available to investors.
