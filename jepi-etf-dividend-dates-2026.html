When Does JEPI Pay Its Dividend in 2026?

The JPMorgan Equity Premium Income ETF ($JEPI) distributes monthly. The ex-dividend date is normally the first business day of the month, the record date falls on the same day, and the pay date lands about two business days later. It runs on the exact same calendar as its Nasdaq sister fund, $JEPQ.

$JEPI's October 2026 distribution is $0.34134 per share, with an ex-date of October 1 and a pay date of October 5. That is an 8.1% step down from September's $0.37142 and the smallest monthly payout since March 2025. The 2026 high so far is May's $0.44761.

Across 2026, $JEPI has paid $3.42 per share over nine distributions, an average of $0.3800 per month, just below 2025's $0.3933. Over the trailing twelve months it has paid $4.56 per share, a yield of roughly 8% at a share price in the mid $50s.

One quirk catches investors out every year: JEPI has no January ex-dividend date. The explanation is below.

⚠️ Why JEPI Has No January Ex-Dividend Date

Look at any JEPI calendar and January appears to be missing. It isn't. JEPI runs two December ex-dividend dates every year: one at the start of December and a second in the final days of the month. That late-December distribution pays in early January, and it is the payment shareholders receive as their January income.

  • Ex Dec 29, 2022 → paid Jan 4, 2023 ($0.57292)
  • Ex Dec 28, 2023 → paid Jan 3, 2024 ($0.42781)
  • Ex Dec 31, 2024 → paid Jan 3, 2025 ($0.39478)
  • Ex Dec 31, 2025 → paid Jan 5, 2026 ($0.42709)

If you buy JEPI in early January expecting a January ex-date, you have already missed it. Expect the same structure at the end of 2026.

Why JEPI's Dividend Changes Every Month

JEPI's payout is variable by design. Knowing why helps you avoid panic when it dips.

JEPI holds an actively managed, lower-volatility portfolio of large U.S. stocks and adds an options overlay, mostly through equity-linked notes that sell call options on the S&P 500. The option premiums, plus dividends from the stocks, fund the monthly distribution.

Option premiums scale with implied volatility. When markets get choppy, calls sell for more and JEPI's payout rises. When markets are calm and grinding higher, premiums shrink and the payout falls. That is why 2022, a volatile year, produced JEPI's biggest payouts, and why calmer 2024 produced its smallest.

A lower JEPI distribution is not a dividend cut in the way a company reducing its dividend is. It reflects a calmer month for the options market.

The Range Since 2022

  • Highest: $0.62102 (July 2022)
  • Lowest: $0.28949 (August 2024)
  • 2026 high: $0.44761 (May 2026)
  • 2026 low: $0.34134 (October 2026)
  • 2026 average so far: $0.3800 per month across 9 payments

The best month has paid more than double the weakest. If you budget real monthly income around JEPI, plan against the average rather than the most recent payment.

💡 How to Qualify for a JEPI Distribution

  • Buy before the ex-dividend date. Purchase no later than the prior trading day. Buying on the ex-date itself means you miss that month.
  • Ex-date and record date are the same day for JEPI, so there is no separate window to track.
  • You can sell on or after the ex-date and still receive the payment. Owning shares before the ex-date is what matters.
  • The pay date lands about two business days later, faster than many monthly ETFs.
